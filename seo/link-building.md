## Best outreach angle

**"The free gamified option missing from your workout-app roundup."** Workout Quest is free, iOS+Android, and genuinely different (RPG progression, XP, streaks, AI coach, offline). Listicle editors covering "best free workout apps" or "gamified fitness apps" get a one-line hook: a free app with a screenshot-friendly hook, already ranked top-10 in Google for "gamified fitness apps free" (position 2.1, 26% CTR in Search Console).

## Highest-priority prospect type

**Free/self-serve directories and listing platforms that already link to us or auto-list similar apps** — they convert without email outreach and compound referral links: SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, App Store editorial-adjacent directories (chrome-stats, indusappstore, apkmoda already link). Second wave: medium-size fitness media with roundup formats (Garage Gym Reviews, recoveryguru, gymandfitness).
